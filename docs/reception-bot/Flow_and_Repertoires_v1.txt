# Hype On Media — Triage Bot: Full Copy System

---

## 1. FLOW TREE — STATE BY STATE

---

### STATE 0 — OPENING (approved, use as-is)

> 👋 Welcome to Hype On Media. We grow YouTube channels done-for-you. Tell us a little and we'll set you up with a free audit. What's your channel built for?
>
> `[Ad revenue]` `[Lead generation]` `[Brand / awareness]` `[Something else]`

---

### STATE 1 — PURPOSE ACKNOWLEDGMENT + HANDLE REQUEST

**After `[Ad revenue]`**

> **Var A:** Revenue-focused channels are where we do our best work. What's your channel handle? It looks like @yourchannelname.
>
> **Var B:** Got it. Ad revenue scales fast when the underlying strategy is solid. Drop your handle and we'll take a look.
>
> **Var C:** Makes sense. Share your YouTube handle and we'll start there.

---

**After `[Lead generation]`**

> **Var A:** YouTube is one of the strongest lead engines out there when it's set up right. What's your channel handle?
>
> **Var B:** Lead gen on YouTube takes a specific approach. Drop your handle and the team will dig in.
>
> **Var C:** Good foundation. The right audit will show exactly where the funnel is leaking. What's your handle?

---

**After `[Brand / awareness]`**

> **Var A:** Brand presence on YouTube pays off across every other channel. What's your handle?
>
> **Var B:** Good starting point. A strong audit will show you where the visibility gaps are. Share your handle.
>
> **Var C:** Got it. Visibility that lasts is worth building carefully. What's your channel handle?

---

**After `[Something else]`**

> **Var A:** No problem. Every channel is its own thing. Drop your handle and the team will look at the full picture.
>
> **Var B:** Works for us. Share your handle and we'll figure out the right angle with fresh eyes.
>
> **Var C:** Fair enough. The team is good at reading channels that don't fit a clean box. What's your handle?

---

### STATE 2 — STANDALONE HANDLE REQUEST

*(Used when the bot needs to ask for the handle without a prior purpose button, e.g. if the user typed a response instead of clicking.)*

> **Var A:** What's your YouTube channel handle? It usually looks like @yourchannelname.
>
> **Var B:** Drop your channel handle and we'll pull it up. Format is @yourchannelname.
>
> **Var C:** Share your YouTube handle and the audit starts there.

---

### STATE 3 — CHANNEL RECOGNITION (API found the channel)

> **Var A:** Got it. I can see [Channel Name] with [X] subscribers. The team will have a lot to work with. What's the best email to send your audit to?
>
> **Var B:** Found it. [Channel Name], [X] subscribers. What email should we use for your audit?
>
> **Var C:** [Channel Name] at [X] subscribers. You're in the right place. Leave us an email and we'll take it from there.

---

### STATE 3B — CHANNEL NOT FOUND

> **Var A:** I couldn't find that handle. Mind double-checking it? It should look like @yourchannelname.
>
> **Var B:** That handle didn't come up. Try entering it as @yourchannelname and we'll pull it right up.

---

### STATE 4 — EMAIL REQUEST

> **Var A:** What's the best email to send your audit to?
>
> **Var B:** What email should the team use?
>
> **Var C:** Last step. What email works for you?

---

### STATE 5 — CAPTURE CONFIRMATION

> **Var A:** Done. The team will review [handle] and send your free audit to [email]. Keep an eye on your inbox.
>
> **Var B:** All set. [handle] is in the queue. Your audit goes to [email].
>
> **Var C:** Perfect. The team has [handle] and will reach out at [email] with your free audit.

---

### STATE 6 — CLOSE / OPTIONAL ADD-ON

> **Var A:** Anything else while you're here?
>
> **Var B:** Is there a specific challenge on your channel you'd like the team to focus on?
>
> **Var C:** One last thing: if there's something specific you want the audit to address, add it here and the team will make sure it's covered.

## 4. NOT AVAILABLE

*For when the bot is down, at capacity, or the daily limit has been reached.*

1. The team isn't available to process new requests right now. Leave your email and channel handle and we'll follow up as soon as possible.

2. We're at capacity at the moment. Drop your email and handle here and the team will be in touch.

3. No one available to take this right now. Leave your email and your YouTube handle and we'll reach out when we're back.

4. We're temporarily paused on new audits. Leave your email and handle and you'll be first in line when we reopen.

5. Fully booked for the moment. Leave your email and channel handle here and the team will reach out when there's an opening.

## 5. SPECIAL CASES

---

### User drops email first, without going through the flow

> **Var A:** Thanks for that. To put together your audit, we also need your YouTube channel handle. What is it?
>
> **Var B:** Got your email. One more thing: your YouTube channel handle. We'll pull it up and the team will be ready when they reach out.

---

### Email invalid or incomplete

> **Var A:** That email doesn't look quite right. Mind double-checking it?
>
> **Var B:** Want to verify that email? We want to make sure the audit reaches you.

---

### User asks for a channel analysis / "what's wrong with my channel"

> **Var A:** That's exactly what the free audit is for. Drop your email and handle and the team will dig in.
>
> **Var B:** That question has a real answer, but it lives in your data. Leave your email and handle and we'll find it.

---

### User asks for a general assistant task (code, essays, translations, etc.)

> **Var A:** We're a YouTube growth agency, so that's outside what we do here. If you have a channel you'd like to grow, the free audit is a good place to start.
>
> **Var B:** That's not something we handle. Hype On Media focuses on YouTube channel growth. If that's relevant to you, leave your email and handle and we'll take a look.

---

### User is frustrated or insistent

> **Var A:** Understood. The team is the right resource for this, not the chat. Leave your email and handle and someone will reach out directly.
>
> **Var B:** Fair point. The best way to get a real answer is to connect you with someone from the team. Email and handle?
>
> **Var C:** Noted. Leave your email and handle and the team will follow up, no runaround.
